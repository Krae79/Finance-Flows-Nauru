Methodology notes. Every figure on this page, including this footer's own project counts, is computed in the browser from the currently loaded rows — nothing is pre-baked. "Who is funding it" is a mention-based reach measure: a line naming three partners credits its full capital to each of the three, so partner totals overlap and do not sum to the grand total. Rows flagged with a Duplicate Status of "Confirmed overlap" are excluded from the adjusted combined total shown in the deep-analysis panel above (see the Duplicates tab); rows flagged "Possible overlap" are shown everywhere but not excluded from any total.
