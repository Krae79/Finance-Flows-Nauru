Past vs Future Insights

Seven findings meant to inform decisions, not repeat the charts already shown on the other tabs.

Sources. Commonwealth Tracker (Past): 85 historical Nauru climate finance projects and activities, spanning 1996 to 2031 start/end years, verified capital $675.08M. RONAdapt II Pipeline (Future): 397 costed, phased line items across 21 named priority activities, transcribed from RONAdapt II (Government of the Republic of Nauru), verified capital $252.87M. Combined across both tracks, $927.95M. Both tracks are read live from data/projects.csv, see README.md.

Methodology notes. Every figure on this page is computed in the browser from the currently loaded rows. Location and Lead Organisation are not recorded for any row in either track and are shown as such rather than inferred. “Who funded it” and “Who is proposed to fund it” are mention-based reach measures, a line naming three partners credits its full capital to each of the three, so partner totals overlap and do not sum to that tab's total. RONAdapt II's Funding Outcome is recorded as “Unspecified” for all 397 lines and its Status is “Proposed” for all 397 lines, this is how the source data records the pipeline, not a gap in this dashboard.
