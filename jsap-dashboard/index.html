      11. Method

      Each Chuuk sub-activity takes the closest defensible published precedent, brought to 2026 prices and scaled to Chuuk; where none exists, the team's expert estimate fills the gap, labelled; where neither is defensible, the line stays open.
      1. Source orderStep 1 Chuuk JSAP 2016 Appendix D. Step 2 Chuuk IDP 2025-2034 Vol. 2, counted in full only where the project is itself adaptation or the NAP asks for its delivery (rule 5). Step 3 other FSM State JSAPs (Pohnpei 2016, Yap 2015, Kosrae 2015), then Pacific plans and projects (Adaptation Fund MCT FSM 2017, Palau PACC 2012, Cook Islands JNAP II). Step 4, for the Chuuk tab only: the team's expert estimate, labelled, where Steps 1 to 3 give no figure.
      2. Prices and running costsUS CPI-U escalation to a 2026 price base (January to September 2026 average, 332.243). Cook Islands costs convert at NZD 1.436526 per USD. OPEX is 2% of CAPEX a year for programmes and 3% for infrastructure (rule 7), applied to team estimates too.
      3. Scale and integrityState-level items are not scaled. Community programmes scale by population (2010 Census: Chuuk 48,654, Pohnpei 36,196, Yap 11,376, Kosrae 6,616). Site works scale by the 5 Chuuk regions. Each Chuuk JSAP 2016 line costs one sub-activity only (rule 6), and covered-elsewhere items carry $0 to avoid double counting.
      original cost × FX × CPI factor × scale = 2026 estimate

      How the figures were checked

      • Every original cost is found in its source text, or is a stated sum or average of figures that are (verify.py, FAILURES: 0).
      • An independent reviewer checked all 168 source lines and recomputed every estimate (3 November 2026); fixes from that review are in the costing.
      • Every Chuuk tab value was compared row by row with the source-based tab and the team's original entries; all match.
      • The workbook's How to check tab gives five steps and live worked examples for checking any figure by hand.

      Team figures not added to the Chuuk tab

      • Resilience shares of IDP development projects (5.2.3 roads, 5.3.4 seaport, 5.4.3 and 5.4.4 airstrip and runway, 6.3.3 hospital design): rule 5 leaves these blank pending a team decision.
      • Figures in Packages 3 and 4 that came from an earlier source-based draft and were removed by the independent review as low confidence or double counting.
      • Team figures for covered-elsewhere items, which would double count.

      Assumptions to confirm

      • Site counts: Kosrae 4, Pohnpei 6, Yap 3 (Chuuk 5 regions).
      • Two priority taro sites per region (3.4.2).
      • Infrastructure OPEX at 3% of CAPEX a year.
      • US CPI-U may understate Pacific construction cost growth.
      • No contingency added. The Yap JSAP adds 10% on its line totals; it is left out here for consistency with the Chuuk and Pohnpei JSAPs.
