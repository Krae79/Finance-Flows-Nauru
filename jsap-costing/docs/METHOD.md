# Method, assumptions and open decisions

## Method

1. Each Chuuk sub-activity is matched to a costed example in this order: (1) Chuuk JSAP 2016 Appendix D; (2) Chuuk IDP 2025-2034 Vol. 2; (3) other FSM State JSAPs (Pohnpei 2016, Yap 2015, Kosrae 2015), then other Pacific plans and projects (Adaptation Fund MCT FSM 2017, Palau PACC 2012, Cook Islands JNAP II 2016-2020).
2. Each match is labelled direct match or analogue. Where no defensible example exists, the sub-activity is left blank (yellow). Where another sub-activity covers it, it is $0 with a note.
3. Estimate (USD 2026) = original cost x FX to USD x (CPI-U 2026 / CPI-U price year) x scale to Chuuk.
4. Scaling: none for State-level items; population ratio for community programmes; number of Chuuk regions (5) for site works and pilots.
5. OPEX = 2% of CAPEX a year (programmes) or 3% (infrastructure).
6. State roll-up: each source line tagged same in every State, population factor, sites factor or State-specific; applied only where the State tab has that activity.

Worked example, 3.2.1: Chuuk JSAP 2016 2.4.4 $342,600 x 1.384 = $474,262; Pohnpei JSAP 2016 nurseries $59,970 x 1.384 x 1.34 = $111,590; maintenance $11,400 x 1.384 x 1.34 = $21,213; total $607,065.

## Checks performed

- Every original cost found in its source text, or a stated sum or average of figures that are (3 derived lines).
- One transcription error corrected (Pohnpei 8.5.3: $7,315 stated sub-total).
- Every sub-activity classified exactly once; independent recompute matches every cell; Worked examples and State factors checks all match.
- Chuuk JSAP 2016 lines used once each (one reuse removed: 1.6.1).
- Order of magnitude: JSAP 2016 appendix total $81.7m (2016), about $113m in 2026 prices; 59% of it used.

## Assumptions to confirm

- Site counts: Kosrae 4, Pohnpei 6, Yap 3 (Chuuk 5 regions).
- Two priority taro sites per region (3.4.2).
- Infrastructure OPEX 3%.
- Pohnpei uses the full NAP activity list (tab not yet populated).
- US CPI-U may understate Pacific construction cost growth.

## Decisions for the team

- Unused JSAP 2016 lines: shelter disability access ($7.8m), dispensary site and survey ($4.3m, identical figures), health education ($1.65m), inland construction ($10m) and relocation transport and land ($4.08m).
- Largest lines: 5.3.2 docks (IDP CE5, CE6, $37.1m), 1.2.2 retrofit ($15.3m), 4.6.4 slope stabilisation ($14.2m), 4.3.7 seawalls ($12.7m).
- 69 not-costed sub-activities, mainly in Packages 2, 5 and 6.
