7. Method

Each Chuuk sub-activity is costed from the closest defensible precedent, brought to 2026 prices and scaled to Chuuk; where no precedent exists, it stays open.
1. Three-step source orderStep 1 Chuuk JSAP 2016 Appendix D. Step 2 Chuuk IDP 2025-2034 Vol. 2, counted in full only where the project is itself adaptation or the NAP asks for its delivery. Step 3 other FSM State JSAPs (Pohnpei 2016, Yap 2015, Kosrae 2015), then Pacific plans and projects (Adaptation Fund MCT FSM 2017, Palau PACC 2012, Cook Islands JNAP II). Each line is labelled direct match or analogue.
2. Escalation to 2026US CPI-U, with the 2026 price base set at the January to September 2026 average (332.243). Cook Islands costs convert at NZD 1.436526 per USD. OPEX is 2% of CAPEX a year for programmes and 3% for infrastructure.
3. Scaling to ChuukState-level items are not scaled. Community programmes scale by population (2010 Census: Chuuk 48,654, Pohnpei 36,196, Yap 11,376, Kosrae 6,616). Site works and pilots scale by the 5 Chuuk regions.
original cost × FX × CPI factor × scale = 2026 estimate
